## Exact prompts

### House

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 2D mobile Chiikawa game home background, landscape 3:2. Create a clean hand-drawn anime-background version of Chiikawa's canonical tiny white dome house INTERIOR. The attached product image is architectural reference only: match the curved white dome wall, simple central arched white door, and tiny pale-blue four-pane window on the right. Eye-level front interior scene, not isometric, not a toy photograph. Plain warm ivory curved walls; low ivory/cream floor. Door in back center with a tiny brass knob; four pane blue window right. At left only a very small rounded built-in recess. Main floor foreground occupies bottom 45% and is EMPTY so game will overlay character and furnishings. Soft pastel colors, very simple flat shading, slightly irregular dark-brown anime outline, friendly Chiikawa animation aesthetic. No people, no characters, no toys, no furniture, no table, no bed, no text, no logos, no speech bubbles, no watermark. Full-bleed opaque landscape background. Do not add a triangular roof, bricks, wooden cottage, or decorative fantasy architecture.

### Cave

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 2D mobile Chiikawa game cave home background, landscape 3:2. Attached official anime still is exact environment reference. Render Hachiware's small cave HOME interior with the same pale gray irregular broad rock wall shapes and warm brown hand-drawn outlines. Keep a low simple warm-brown two-drawer cabinet at far left with a few vertical green/cream books and pink rectangular box on top; a tiny floating narrow wooden shelf near upper middle with one small bottle. At far right an irregular rocky cave doorway reveals bright pale blue daylight and a narrow patch of grass. Empty spacious light gray floor occupies bottom 45%, clear center for game character and movable furnishing overlays. Remove Hachiware, camera, bowl, center box/table, sacks, all characters and objects in foreground. Plain clean hand-drawn flat soft anime palette, slightly irregular dark-brown outlines, frontal eye level like reference, no isometric. No text, copyright writing, watermark, extra creatures. Full-bleed opaque landscape background.

### Car

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: 2D mobile Chiikawa game background, landscape 3:2. Attached official sticker is exact shape reference for Rakko's tiny car. Draw ONLY the actual small rounded WHITE car from the reference, faithfully preserve its charming domed bubble canopy, rounded front with two pink circle headlights like cheeks, tiny black eyes and curved eyebrow details, little rectangular grille above smiling mouth, two black wheels, and small five-petal pink flower perched on roof on a short stem. White body with the palest blue window reflection; do not replace this with an ordinary car or house. Car parked in back-left-to-middle of scene, three-quarter front view facing left as reference. Leave lower 40% grass and sandy dirt path EMPTY for the game character and moveable objects. Landscape gentle meadow, a few low green bushes in far distance and pale blue sky. Hand-drawn Chiikawa anime aesthetic, flat pastel fill, slightly irregular dark-brown outline, minimal soft shade, same proportions and shape as reference. No Rakko, no animals, no people, no characters, no text, no watermark, no car brand. Full-bleed opaque landscape.
